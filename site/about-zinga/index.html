About Zinga

Friendly advisers. Straight answers. The right mortgage.

Our story

Zinga Financial Services is an FCA-regulated mortgage and protection broker at 1st Floor, 192 High Street, Uxbridge UB8 1LB. We help first-time buyers, home movers, remortgagers, landlords and business owners across London and beyond.

We started with a simple idea: clients should know exactly who is looking after them, and hear back quickly when they get in touch. That's why every client has a dedicated adviser and case handler — making your journey part of ours too.

01

A dedicated adviser and case handler

Your adviser recommends the mortgage; your case handler chases the lender, solicitor and agent. You’ll have both names and direct numbers from day one.

02

A reply within two working hours

Whether you call, message on WhatsApp or request a callback, someone from your team will reply within two working hours.

03

Access to a wide range of lenders

We work with an unrestricted number of lenders, so we can look beyond the high street when your circumstances need it.

Fees and commission

How we work and how we're paid.

You'll know what advice costs before you commit. We explain any fee, and the commission we receive from lenders or insurers, in plain language and in writing.

  • First conversationNo charge and no obligation. We learn about your plans and explain how we can help.
  • Advice feeIf a fee applies to your case, we tell you the amount and when it’s payable before any work starts.
  • CommissionWe receive commission from lenders and insurers. Details of the model, calculation and amount are disclosed throughout your journey.
  • Other costsLender fees, valuations, legal costs and Stamp Duty are separate — we’ll list what applies to you.
