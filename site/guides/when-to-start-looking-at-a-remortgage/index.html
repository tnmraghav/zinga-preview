When should I start looking at a remortgage?

By Arifa Shakoor · Reviewed by Seema Babber
20 September 2026 · 5 min read

Share WhatsApp LinkedIn Facebook X Email

Quick summary

  • Start around six months before your current deal ends.
  • Many lenders let you secure a new rate that far ahead, which gives you time to compare options and avoid moving onto your lender’s standard variable rate.
Table of contents
  1. Why six months?

Why six months?

Most mortgage offers are valid for around six months. Applying early means the new rate can start the day your current deal ends, and you can usually still switch if a better-suited option appears before completion.

Every lender's rules are different. We compare them against your circumstances, so you don't have to.

Request a callback Call 0208 497 8737

What to check first

  • The exact date your current deal ends
  • Any early repayment charge if you leave before that date
  • Your outstanding balance and an estimate of your home’s value

Your two main routes

  1. Product transfer — a new deal with your current lender, usually with fewer checks.
  2. Remortgage to a new lender — a full application that may suit your circumstances better.

Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage.
