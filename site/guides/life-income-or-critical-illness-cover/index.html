Life, income or critical illness cover — what is the difference?

By the Zinga Financial Services team
24 September 2026 · 5 min read

Share WhatsApp LinkedIn Facebook X Email

Quick summary

  • Life insurance pays out if you die, critical illness cover pays a lump sum if you’re diagnosed with a specified serious illness, and income protection pays a monthly amount if you can’t work.
  • Many households combine them.

Life insurance

Pays a lump sum to your family if you die during the policy term. Often arranged to match the mortgage balance and term.

Every lender's rules are different. We compare them against your circumstances, so you don't have to.

Request a callback Call 0208 497 8737

Critical illness cover

Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of a condition listed in the policy. Definitions vary between insurers.

Income protection

Replaces part of your income, usually monthly, if illness or injury stops you working, after a waiting period you choose.

Policies have exclusions and conditions. Cover may not pay out in every circumstance.
