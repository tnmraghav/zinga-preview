FAQ

Questions we hear every day.

Still unsure? Call an adviser.

01Do I need a mortgage adviser?

You don't have to use one. An adviser compares lenders' criteria against your circumstances, recommends a suitable option and manages the application with the lender, solicitor and estate agent.

02What does your advice cost?

We'll explain any fee and how we're paid — including commission from lenders — before you commit to anything, and confirm it in writing.

03How much can I borrow?

Lenders look at your income, outgoings, credit history and deposit, and criteria vary between them. We can give you a realistic figure in your first conversation.

04Do you only work with clients in Uxbridge?

No. Our office is in Uxbridge and we advise clients across Ruislip, Slough, Hounslow and beyond — by phone, video or in person.
