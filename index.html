Homepage design review

Website redesign

Four hero options

As discussed on 8 October, here is the simplified homepage with four choices for the top of the page: a looping video or a photo, each either below the menu or running up behind it. Each sits behind a dark overlay, and the rest of the page is the same in all four. Both work on desktop and mobile. Only the homepage is included in this preview, and the enquiry form doesn't send anything.

Option A · 9 Oct

Video background

A slow, silent loop of home and advice scenes plays behind the heading and motto. It pauses for visitors who prefer reduced motion and has a pause button. Our sample is made from the site's illustrative images; a filmed or licensed clip can replace it.

View option A →
Option B · 9 Oct

Photo background

One full-width photo behind the message: lighter to load and calmer than a video. The photo can be changed at any time, ideally to a real photo of the Zinga team or office.

View option B →
Option C · 9 Oct

Photo behind the menu

The same photo, but it runs right up behind the menu bar, which turns transparent with a white logo. It switches back to the normal white menu as soon as you scroll or open a menu. Option D shows the same with the video.

View option C →
Option D · 9 Oct

Video behind the menu

Option A's video, running right up behind the transparent menu bar with a white logo. Like option C, the menu turns white again as soon as you scroll or open a menu.

View option D →

What changed since the call: the line above the heading and the FCA number in the top bar are removed; the repeated sections are gone (“Not just a comparison site”, “What can we help with?”, the three steps, advisers and protection — they now live on their own pages); About Zinga shows lenders, Google and Trustpilot, with the whole-of-market, dedicated adviser and end-to-end points underneath; and the calculators sit higher up. The hero now uses your wording: “Every mortgage has a purpose. We take the time to understand yours.” with the “Talk to an adviser” button.
