Chapter 01

01

The short answer on remortgaging

When a fixed or tracker deal ends, most lenders move you onto their standard variable rate, which is often higher. You can usually secure a new deal up to six months in advance — either with your current lender (a product transfer) or by moving to a new one. We compare both and explain any costs of switching.

Who it's for

Homeowners whose deal ends within the next six months, anyone wanting to borrow more for improvements, or those wanting to change the term or add or remove a name.

Chapter 04

04

Remortgage costs and risks

What to budget for

  • Early repayment charge, if leaving before your deal ends
  • Lender product or arrangement fees
  • Legal and valuation costs (often included by lenders)
  • Our fee, if any — explained in writing first

Important

Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage.

07

Questions about remortgaging

Didn't find your answer? An adviser can help.

Request a callback
How early can I remortgage?

Many lenders let you secure a new rate up to six months before your current deal ends.

Will I pay an early repayment charge?

Only if you leave before your current deal ends. We check your mortgage terms before recommending anything.

Can I remortgage to borrow more?

Often, yes — subject to affordability, your property value and what the money is for.
